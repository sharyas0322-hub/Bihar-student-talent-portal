Frequently asked questions

Who can register?

Students who meet the programme's published eligibility requirements can register. Final eligibility rules can be configured by the programme administrator.

Can I register for Sports and Cultural activities?

The portal can support one or multiple registrations depending on the programme rules.

How will I track my application?

Use your mobile number as User ID and your initial date of birth as password in the demo. The live system should require a secure password change after first login.
